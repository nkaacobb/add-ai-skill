# Proofreading checklist

Work through the text once per group; report each problem once with how many times it occurs.

## Spelling

- Misspelt words and typos (doubled or swapped letters: "teh", "recieve", "seperate").
- Homophones in the wrong place: their / there / they're, your / you're, its / it's, then / than, to / too.
- One spelling system throughout (British "colour, organise" or American "color, organize"); follow the user's
  saved preference, else the one most of the text uses.

## Grammar

- Subject–verb agreement ("the list of items are" → "is").
- Tense consistency within a paragraph.
- Articles: a / an before vowel sounds ("an hour", "a user").
- Sentence fragments and run-on sentences.

## Punctuation

- Missing full stops; doubled spaces or punctuation (",,", "..", "  ").
- Commas after introductory phrases and around non-essential clauses; no comma splices.
- Matching quotation marks and brackets.

## Consistency

- Names, terms and capitalisation spelt the same way everywhere.
- Numbers: figures or words, one style; units with a space ("5 km").
- List items in the same form (all sentences, or all fragments).

Do not change: deliberate style (sentence fragments used for effect), quotations, code, file names, URLs.
